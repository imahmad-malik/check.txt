THE SHORT-HAIR NOTES

The site direction.

This preview demonstrates the homepage design and visitor tools. It is not the finished WordPress package or the 100-look launch library.

Core collection intent

Bixie haircut, pixie-bob and closely related short-shape references. Separate useful edits by length, texture, density, fringe and finish.

Editorial support

What is a bixie, pixie vs. bob, stylist conversations, styling, fine vs. thin hair, three-angle references and grow-out.

Production boundaries

Meaningful collection pages need enough distinct looks and useful copy. Avoid empty pages and hundreds of indexed filter combinations.

Read the accompanying research files for measured export terms, competitor rows and the proposed page map. Search volume and rankings are source estimates at their exported snapshots; no ranking outcome is guaranteed.
