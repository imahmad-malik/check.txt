# Bixie Haircut: supplied-keyword review and coverage plan

**Historical research note:** this analysis was first prepared during the HTML-prototype phase. The current deliverable is the WordPress package described in `../CONTENT-COVERAGE.md`; its scope and strict publication/media requirements supersede the old prototype-specific recommendations below. Export counts and source observations remain applicable.

This review informs an **HTML design prototype** for Bixie Haircut. It does not authorize building or delivering a WordPress theme. The older master prompt supplies brand and content context; the latest user instruction controls the deliverable.

## What the supplied files establish

- Seven US-labelled Semrush exports contain **13,907 rows** and **10,851 distinct normalized keywords**.
- The two Laura K Collins exports overlap by **207 identical rows**. They contain **1,169 distinct keywords in union**, rather than 1,500 distinct keywords. The reproducible analysis combines observations and retains different URLs, SERP slots and crawl dates.
- Direct matching finds **51 distinct bixie / pixie-bob / comparison terms in 104 observations**, including **39 terms explicitly containing bixie/bixies**. Excluding two celebrity-specific terms and the broad “bobs and pixie cuts” phrase leaves **48 direct or equivalent-name candidates**. One is historical “bixie haircut 2025”; it is not a new 2026 landing-page target.
- A further **30 supporting candidates** relate to styling, growing out short hair, or explaining strand diameter versus density. They belong in relevant guides, not 30 thin articles.
- Exact relevant terms appear in John Frieda, Neal & Wolf, Stylist and Keyword Magic. Neither Laura export nor the supplied Good Housekeeping excerpt contains an exact bixie/pixie-bob term. Their surrounding hair topics offer selective supporting context.
- These are partial exports. A term missing from them is **not evidence of zero demand**. They do not establish every keyword or every competitor.

## Dates and ranking interpretation

The filenames say 2026-10-06, but the Keyword Magic rows use **Snapshot Date 20260801**. Competitor rows contain their own crawl times. Keep the two data sources distinct instead of silently selecting whichever number is larger.

For `bixie haircut`, the exported organic rows (`positionIsSERPFeature=false`) show **Neal & Wolf 1**, **John Frieda 2** and **Stylist 5**, with core crawl time 2026-10-06 01:15:00. Feature observations also show position 1; those are feature slots and must not be reported as additional organic #1 results. This is a reading of the supplied exports, **not a fresh live Google US ranking audit**.

Semrush volume and difficulty are export estimates. Volume is not forecast site traffic. Synonyms may overlap, so summing them would overstate distinct demand. High coverage, original images, a premium design, an exact-match domain and animation do not guarantee ranking above every competitor.

## Site coverage map

There should be a canonical page for a useful subject, supported by complete relevant looks and differentiated copy. Spelling variants and reordered phrases should not each become pages. Combining every colour × age × texture × length creates many weak pages; use filters for those narrow combinations.

The prototype can show the **entire collection directory and all browsing dimensions** while distinguishing finished preview assets from planned production. For the future real site, start with evidence-backed and sufficiently populated pages; publish the remaining editorial collections once their distinct imagery and copy exist. Do not label an empty planned gallery as complete.

## Supporting guides and trust pages

1. **What is a bixie?** Define the shape and compare pixie, bob and boy bob. Answer the measured question variants in one canonical guide.
2. **What to ask your stylist.** Front/side/back references, crown, nape, face-framing pieces, fringe and expected upkeep. Do not pretend the site has salon experience.
3. **Styling a bixie.** Straight, wavy and curly approaches; practical easy-styling tradeoffs. `how to style short hair` is a broader supporting query (Keyword Magic volume 5,400, difficulty 47), not proof a bixie guide will rank for the whole short-hair category.
4. **Fine hair versus thin hair.** Strand diameter versus density, with careful fine/coarse explanation. Exact `fine vs coarse hair` (positions volume 880/KD19) and `coarse vs fine hair` (390/KD12) support contextual explanation; do not write 30 repetitive texture articles.
5. **How to read three-angle references.** Shape, fringe, side silhouette and graduated nape; explain that generated concepts require discussion with a stylist.
6. **Growing out a bixie.** Maintenance and shaping choices. `growing out a pixie cut` appears in Keyword Magic (2,400/difficulty28), but a pixie is not automatically a bixie.
7. **Bixie, shixie and wolf cut.** A focused optional comparison if differentiated copy and references justify a separate guide; otherwise place a clear section in the main comparison guide.

Essential visible site destinations: About, Contact, Image Policy, Privacy and Disclaimer, plus accessible Search, Saved, Compare, Print and 404/empty states. Contact details require the owner’s real address; do not invent one. A future production privacy policy must reflect actual storage, analytics and forms.

## Visual implications for this prototype

- Build a long **gallery-first** home with a strong original typographic identity, film-like photographic movement, multiple editorial rhythms, a real collection directory, a three-angle reference feature, mature/grey collections, guides and complete trust/footer navigation.
- Use each displayed photograph **once in the homepage composition**, with stable unique asset IDs and distinct photos per section. Moving rails should not clone the same photos into multiple visible tiles. Pause/resume and reduced-motion support matter more than animation quantity.
- A single canonical look may eventually appear in multiple relevant collection results; that is information architecture, not evidence of an SEO penalty. Do not tell the user repeated photography inherently prevents ranking. The requested homepage can nevertheless avoid repeats entirely.
- Show filters for texture, density, length, fringe, finish, age reference, face-shape reference and colour; keep filter combinations from generating an unlimited search-indexed URL space.
- Do not show competitor metrics inside the consumer homepage. Keep strategy and verification information in documentation.
- The latest instruction asks for video flow. If no real original video is produced, disclose that image motion is a photographic animation, rather than labelling still-image motion as completed video production.

## Future SEO and verification boundaries

This research did not fetch live Google US or image results, backlinks, competitor full articles or Search Console data. It does not establish a current top-five SERP audit, measured site speed, rankings, traffic or field Core Web Vitals. The HTML prototype should remain preview/noindex until the owner approves a production implementation.

Future production requirements include unique useful page titles/descriptions, real image-specific alt text, crawlable links and image elements, coherent front/side/back records, self-canonicals for pagination, a sensible indexability matrix, truthful structured data, image sitemap coverage, performance measurement and Search Console monitoring. Homepage visual polish alone cannot verify these production requirements.

## Reproducible files

- `analyze_semrush.py`: analysis of all seven original attachments; run with Python 3.
- `export_inventory.csv`: per-file rows, unique counts, match counts and actual data dates.
- `summary.json`: aggregate counts and limitations.
- `direct_keyword_observations.csv`: all 104 direct observations, including organic/feature flags, source files, exact metrics and exported URLs.
- `bixie_keyword_coverage.csv`: all 51 direct/equivalent/comparison terms, with source-specific volume/difficulty, organic positions and suggested canonical destinations.
- `supporting_keyword_candidates.csv`: 30 selected contextual guide candidates.
- `all_keywords_review.csv`: all 10,851 unique normalized terms with conservative rule-based relevance classification. This is a triage artifact, not a claim that every broad hair term should become a page.

Review method: normalized whitespace/case; exact-row deduplication for same source; direct bixie/pixie-bob match plus documented conservative contextual rules. Historical, celebrity and broad-comparison intent remains visible in the review so exclusion is auditable.
